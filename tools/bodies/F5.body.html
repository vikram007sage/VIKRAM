How the macro links work

02 / FIXED INCOME

Bonds and currencies

Price and yieldMove in opposite directions. A bond paying fixed coupons is worth less when new bonds pay more.
DurationApproximate % price change for a 1% yield change. ΔP ≈ −Duration × Δy: a 7-year-duration bond loses ~7% if yields rise 1%; ~1.75% for a 25 bp rise.
Yield curveNormally upward sloping; inverted (short > long) often precedes recessions; flattening when the central bank hikes.
Credit spreadCorporate yield − G-sec yield; widens in stress.
Interest-rate parityForward rate ≈ spot × (1 + iINR) ÷ (1 + iUSD). With rupee rates higher than dollar rates, the rupee trades at a forward discount.
Index inclusionIndian G-secs joined JP Morgan's EM bond index from 2024 (and later FTSE); Bloomberg deferred its decision in 2026 — inclusion brings passive foreign inflows.
