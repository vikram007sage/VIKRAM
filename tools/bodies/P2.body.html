01 / METRICS

Choosing and defining metrics

core
North-star metricOne number that best captures value delivered to users and predicts long-term revenue. Spotify: time spent listening; Airbnb: nights booked; a UPI app: successful transactions per monthly active user; a card issuer: active spenders × spend per spender.
Input metricsDrivers the team can move that add up to the north star (acquisition, activation, frequency, value per use).
Guardrail metricsThings that must not get worse: latency, crash rate, complaints, fraud, unsubscribes, credit losses, cannibalisation.
Define precisely"Weekly active user = logged-in user with ≥1 meaningful action (payment, search, order) in a 7-day window, excluding employees and bots." Name the numerator, denominator, time window and filters.

Good metric traits: moves with real user value, sensitive enough to detect change in weeks, hard to game, understandable by the team. Vanity metrics (downloads, total registered users, page views) fail at least one.
02 / RCA

Root cause analysis: "metric X dropped — why?"

most asked
1. Clarify the metricExact definition; how much did it drop (absolute and %)? Over what period? Sudden or gradual? Compared with what (last week, same week last year)?
2. Check the dataTracking change, logging bug, dashboard filter, delayed pipeline, definition change. A large share of real drops are instrumentation.
3. External factorsSeasonality and holidays (Diwali, exam season, month-end salary cycle), competitor launch or offer, regulation (RBI rule), outage of a partner (UPI network, bank, payment gateway), app-store issues, news.
4. Internal changesRecent release, experiment, pricing change, marketing spend cut, policy change (KYC, credit rules), ops issue (delivery partners, inventory).
5. SegmentPlatform (Android/iOS/web), app version, geography, new vs existing users, channel, device, user cohort, merchant category. Find where the drop is concentrated.
6. DecomposeUse the metric tree / funnel: which step fell? (traffic, conversion, frequency, AOV).
7. Hypothesise + validateState 2–3 hypotheses ranked by likelihood; name the data that confirms or rejects each.
8. Fix + preventShort-term fix, long-term fix, alert/monitor so it's caught earlier next time.
Say this early: "Before diagnosing user behaviour, I'd rule out a data issue and any external event, then segment to see where the drop is concentrated." Interviewers mark you down for jumping straight to a product theory.
03 / EXPERIMENTS

Experiment design template

core
Hypothesis"Showing the delivery time on the home screen will increase order conversion because users drop when they discover delays at checkout."
Unit of randomisationUser (usual), session, device, city/store (when users interact, e.g. marketplaces, to avoid spillover).
Primary metricOne metric the decision rests on (conversion per visitor).
Secondary + guardrailsAOV, cancellations, latency, complaints, revenue per user.
Sample size + durationFrom baseline, minimum detectable effect, α 5%, power 80% (D3: 10%→11% needs ≈14,751 users per arm). Run full weeks to cover day-of-week patterns.
Decision ruleShip if primary significantly up and no guardrail significantly worse; written before launch.
ThreatsNovelty effect, sample-ratio mismatch, peeking, network effects, multiple metrics, seasonality.
When A/B isn't possibleAlternative
Marketplace with spilloversGeo or switchback tests (alternate time slots by city)
Pricing seen publiclyCity-level tests, difference-in-differences
Too few users (B2B)Before/after with controls, qualitative interviews, staged rollout
Already launchedDifference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, synthetic control
04 / PRACTICE

Ten worked cases

try first
1 · UPI app: successful transactions down 15% yesterday

Data: check success vs initiated; tracking release. External: NPCI or a large bank's servers down (check bank-wise success rate — concentrated in one bank points to a partner outage), holiday. Internal: app release causing failures on one Android version. Segment by bank, app version, P2P vs P2M. Fix: route messages, show bank-down banner, alert on bank-wise success rate.

2 · Quick-commerce: orders per user down 8% over a month

Gradual → not a bug. Check: competitor offers (Instamart/Zepto discounts), our delivery-fee change, stock-outs (fill rate), delivery-time increase (rain, rider shortage), cohort mix (new users from a campaign order less). Decompose: visits per user × conversion × orders. Validate with fill-rate and ETA by store.

3 · Credit card: spend per active card down 10%

Check: seasonality (post-festive dip), reward devaluation (users moved spend to other cards), MCC changes (rent/wallet loads excluded from rewards), credit-limit cuts, decline-rate increase (fraud rules), new-card mix. Segment by MCC, tenure, limit band. Guardrail: delinquency.

4 · Food delivery: app-open to order conversion down on iOS only

Platform-specific → likely release or OS update. Check iOS version split, crash logs, payment-gateway failures on iOS, Apple Pay/UPI intent changes. Roll back or hot-fix.

5 · Metrics for WhatsApp Pay / UPI in a chat app

North star: monthly transacting users. Inputs: activation (first payment within 7 days of onboarding), frequency, P2M share. Guardrails: failure rate, fraud complaints, chat engagement (don't hurt core product).

6 · Metrics for LinkedIn "Open to Work"

Goal: help seekers get hired without hurting recruiter experience. Adoption among job seekers; recruiter InMails to flagged members; response rate; hires attributed. Guardrail: stigma signals (profile hides), spam reports.

7 · Test free delivery above ₹199 vs ₹299

City-level or user-level test; primary: contribution margin per user (not orders alone); secondary: orders, AOV; guardrail: retention. Expect AOV bunching just above the threshold.

8 · Test a new onboarding flow (CheQ-style)

Unit: new user; primary: % completing KYC and linking a card within 7 days; guardrail: drop-off at each step, support tickets; check SRM, run 2 full weeks.

9 · DAU up 20% but revenue flat

Possible: low-value users from a campaign, engagement feature not tied to purchase, discounting, bots. Segment new vs existing; check revenue per DAU; check ARPU by cohort.

10 · Experiment won but metric fell after launch

Novelty effect, winner's curse (overestimated effect), seasonality, interaction with other launches, test population not representative. Use holdback groups to measure long-run effect.
