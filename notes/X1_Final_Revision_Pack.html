04 / RESILIENCE

After a bad round

10 minutesWrite what went wrong in 3 bullets (one fact each, not a judgment of yourself).
Fix one thingPick the single most fixable issue and practise it once before the next round.
ResetWalk, eat, talk to a friend; the next company doesn't know about the last round.
PerspectivePlacement outcomes depend partly on luck and slot timing. Many people who struggle on day 1 get good offers later; keep applying the process.
If stress starts affecting sleep or mood for more than a few days, talk to someone you trust or the campus counselling centre — it's a normal and smart thing to do in placement season.
