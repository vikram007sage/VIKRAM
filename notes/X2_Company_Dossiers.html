Before each test or interview you need a one-page view of the company: what it does, how it earns, what's happening to it now, what the selection process looks like, and why you fit. This module gives the dossier template, process notes for the firm types that recruit for your lanes at IIT Kharagpur (consulting, product, analytics, finance, quant), 2026 updates on assessment formats, and a slot-shortlist method.

00 / ORIENTATION

Why dossiers win interviews

Most candidates answer "why us?" generically. A specific answer — a recent result, a product decision, a strategic bet, and how your experience connects — is the cheapest way to stand out. Build one page per company the week its shortlist or test is announced.

Important: exact processes change every year and differ by campus. Confirm the current format with CDC notices, the company's pre-placement talk, and seniors who interviewed last season. Treat the process notes below as a starting point, not a guarantee.
01 / TEMPLATE

One-page dossier template

core
1. SnapshotWhat it does, segments, size (revenue, users), business model, main competitors.
2. NumbersLatest results: revenue growth, profitability, key operating metric (for a bank: NIM, GNPA; for a platform: GMV, users; for a consultancy: practice areas).
3. Now3 recent developments (launches, deals, regulation, leadership) with dates.
4. RoleJob description in your words; skills it tests; team.
5. ProcessTest type, rounds, typical questions (from seniors and casebooks).
6. Why meTwo links between your experience and their needs (e.g. CheQ A/B testing → product analytics role).
7. Smart questionsTwo questions for the interviewer that show you've done the work.
8. Prep linksWhich VIKRAM modules to revise (e.g. B1 + D1 + P2 for a card-issuer analyst role).

05 / STRATEGY

Building your slot shortlist

core
1. ListAll companies from last year's placement and internship data (CDC records, seniors) that recruit for your lanes and accept your department.
2. ScoreFit (how well your CV matches: 1–5), interest (1–5), past shortlists from your CGPA band and department, slot (day 1, day 2…).
3. TierDream (low odds, high interest), target (good fit), safe (high fit, good odds). Aim for a spread in each slot.
4. Map prepFor each target, the VIKRAM modules to revise and the dossier deadline.
5. RulesRead CDC's application and offer rules carefully (e.g. what happens once you accept an offer) — confirm with the current CDC notice.
Your CGPA (7.91): some firms use cut-offs (often 7.5 or 8.0 — varies). Prioritise firms where your CheQ, KiFayti, case-competition gold and analyst experience outweigh grades, and prepare a crisp answer on CGPA (S3).
