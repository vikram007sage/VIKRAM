01 / HIGH YIELD

Percentages, profit and loss

core

"More than" vs "less than"

If A is x% more than B, then B is less than A by x/(100 + x) × 100%.

A is 25% more than B → B is 25/125 = 20% less than A

Percentage points ≠ percent: 10% → 12% is +2 points but +20%.

Markup and discount

SP = CP × (1 + m) × (1 − d)

Marked 25% above cost, 12% discount: 1.25 × 0.88 = 1.10 → 10% profit. Profit % is on cost price; discount % is on marked price.

Successive discounts

20% then 10% = 1 − 0.8 × 0.9 = 28%, not 30%.

Dishonest dealer

Uses a 900 g weight for 1 kg and sells at cost: gain = 100/900 = 11.11%. Gain % = error ÷ (true value − error).

Trap: "Loss of 20% on SP" vs "on CP". Always ask which base. Default in Indian aptitude tests: profit and loss on cost price.
02 / HIGH YIELD

Ratio, averages and mixtures

Partnership

profit share ∝ capital × time

A puts in ₹50,000 for 12 months, B ₹75,000 for 8 months → 6,00,000 : 6,00,000 = 1 : 1.

Average with a newcomer

newcomer = new total − old total

30 students average 15 years; with the teacher the average is 16 → teacher = 31 × 16 − 30 × 15 = 46.

Alligation

cheap : dear = (dear − mean) : (mean − cheap)

Works for prices, concentrations, speeds: any weighted average of two groups.

Repeated dilution

left = initial × (1 − x/V)ⁿ

40 L of milk; 4 L replaced with water, twice → 40 × 0.9² = 32.4 L of milk.

03 / HIGH YIELD

Time, speed, distance

Relative speed

opposite directions: s₁ + s₂
same direction: s₁ − s₂

Trains crossing each other cover the sum of their lengths.

150 m at 60 km/h vs 100 m at 30 km/h, opposite
90 km/h = 25 m/s → 250 ÷ 25 = 10 s

Boats and streams

down = b + s · up = b − s
b = (down + up)/2 · s = (down − up)/2

Down 15, up 9 → boat 12, stream 3 km/h.

Units

km/h × 5/18 = m/s. Multiples of 18 km/h give clean m/s (36 → 10, 54 → 15, 72 → 20).

Average speed

Equal distances: 2uv/(u + v). Equal times: (u + v)/2. Mixing these up is the most common TSD error.

04 / HIGH YIELD

Time & work, pipes & cisterns

Rates add

1/T = 1/a + 1/b (− 1/c for a leak)

A pipe fills a tank in 6 h, a leak empties it in 9 h → 1/6 − 1/9 = 1/18 → 18 h.

Efficiency (LCM) method

Let total work = LCM of the days. A in 12, B in 18 → total 36 units; A does 3/day, B 2/day → together 5/day → 36/5 = 7.2 days. Faster with three or more people.

Wages

Pay is split in proportion to work done, not days present.

Working backwards

A + B in 12 days, A alone in 20 → B = 1/12 − 1/20 = 1/30 → 30 days.

06 / NUMBERS

Number system

Number of factors

Write N = pᵃ qᵇ rᶜ; factors = (a+1)(b+1)(c+1).

360 = 2³·3²·5 → 4·3·2 = 24

Units digit cycles

Powers repeat every 4 (2: 2,4,8,6; 3: 3,9,7,1; 7: 7,9,3,1; 8: 8,4,2,6). Use exponent mod 4 (0 → 4th term).

7⁴⁵ → 45 mod 4 = 1 → 7

Trailing zeros of n!

⌊n/5⌋ + ⌊n/25⌋ + ⌊n/125⌋ …

100! → 20 + 4 = 24 zeros.

Remainders

Reduce the base first: 17²³ ÷ 16 → 17 ≡ 1 → remainder 1. For (a − 1)ⁿ ÷ a: remainder 1 if n is even, a − 1 if odd.

HCF × LCM

For two numbers, HCF × LCM = product. HCF 12, LCM 360, one number 72 → other = 12 × 360 ÷ 72 = 60.

08 / FORMULA RECALL

Geometry, mensuration and clocks

Triangles

  • Area ½bh; equilateral √3/4·a²
  • Triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25
  • Interior angles of an n-gon: (n − 2) × 180°

Circles and solids

  • Circle: πr², 2πr
  • Cylinder: πr²h; curved area 2πrh
  • Cone: ⅓πr²h · Sphere: 4/3πr³, 4πr²

Clock angle

angle = |30H − 5.5M| (take 360 − angle if > 180)

3:40 → |90 − 220| = 130°. The hands overlap every 65 5/11 minutes.

Last 10-minute look

Six formulas

  1. SP = CP(1 + m)(1 − d)
  2. CI − SI (2 yrs) = P(r/100)²
  3. Average speed (equal distances) = 2uv/(u+v)
  4. 1/T = Σ rates
  5. Factors = Π(exponent + 1)
  6. Trailing zeros = Σ ⌊n/5ᵏ⌋

Five traps

  • Wrong base in % change
  • Adding discounts
  • Arithmetic mean for average speed
  • Forgetting train lengths
  • Sign flip in inequalities
